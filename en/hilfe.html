🛟 Help

Tap a question – the answer opens.

📲 Install Wuzzo
  1. Send your Play Store Gmail to the Wuzzo team (you'll be added as a tester).
  2. Tap here or scan the QR code on the home page.
  3. "Accept" → "Download test app" → "Install".
  4. Open Wuzzo, sign in with your email – done.
🔄 Get updates
  1. Open Wuzzo in the Play Store.
  2. Top right ⋮ → tick "Enable auto update".
  3. Right now: in Wuzzo Profile → ❓ Help & news → "Check for updates".

Updates usually arrive at night (Wi-Fi + charging). No need to delete anything.

🗑️ Deleted Wuzzo – reinstall
  1. Tap here → "Download test app" → "Install".
  2. Sign in with the same email – account, contacts and chats are back.
  3. With PIN backup: enter your PIN – old messages are readable again.
⚠️ "App not available" / "Not found"
  1. In the Play Store tap your letter at the top right – which Google account is signed in?
  2. That Gmail must be added as a tester by the Wuzzo team – otherwise switch accounts or send the address.
  3. Wait 10–30 minutes and try again.
📦 Old Wuzzo (0.26 or older)
  1. Settings → Apps → Wuzzo → Uninstall (only needed once).
  2. Reinstall as above – sign in with the same email.

Wuzzo from Google Play (0.27 or newer)? Then do not delete it, just update.

🧰 Everyday helpers & SOS
  1. In Wuzzo: Profile → 🧰 Everyday helpers – letter explainer, interpreter, calendar, tasks, emergency pass and more.
  2. SOS: top left in your chats, or long-press the Wuzzo icon.
  3. Add emergency numbers: Profile → 💚 Family & SOS.
🌍 Change the app language
  1. In Wuzzo: Profile → Edit profile.
  2. Pick your language under "My language".
  3. Save – the whole app switches right away.
🔐 Back up chats & new phone
  1. In Wuzzo: Profile → 🔒 Privacy & backup → 🔐 Chat backup (PIN) – choose your own PIN.
  2. New phone: Profile → 🔒 Privacy & backup → Moving phones.
