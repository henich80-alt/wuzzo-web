Frequently asked questions

Are my messages safe?

Yes. One-to-one chats are end-to-end encrypted – only the two of you can read them.

How does translation work?

Messages are translated right on your phone – free, unlimited and even offline.

How do I pay?

Securely through Google Play – the way you set it up with Google. Wuzzo never sees your payment details.

Is Wuzzo available for iPhone or PC?

The iPhone version is coming. On your PC, Wuzzo already works at wuzzo.de/pc.
